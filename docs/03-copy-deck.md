# SYS — Copy Deck

The voice, the vocabulary rules, and the exact words used across the site.

---

## 1. Voice

Warm, direct, and confident without performance. Short sentences. Written as though the
designer is speaking to one person, because she is.

- **Say "you" and "we".** Never "our valued clients" or "the customer".
- **No exclamation marks.** Ever.
- **No superlatives we cannot prove.** Not "India's finest". Not "world-class".
- **No filler luxury adjectives.** "Exquisite", "opulent", "regal", "royal" are banned.
- **Sentence case** for headlines. Uppercase only for the small eyebrow labels.
- **Specific beats grand.** "Stitched to your measurements in Hyderabad" beats "crafted with
  unparalleled artistry".

---

## 2. Vocabulary rules

These are enforced, not preferred. They are what separates a fashion brand from a tailoring
service.

| Never say | Always say |
| --- | --- |
| Shop Now / Buy Now | **Start Your Story** |
| Book Tailor / Get Stitching | Start Your Story · Book a consultation |
| Add to cart | **Customize this design** |
| Products | **Stories We've Styled** |
| My Orders | **My Stories** |
| Order #12345 | The story's name — *My Sister's Wedding* |
| Customers / Clients (in UI) | You · your story |
| Categories | What are you dressing for? |
| Checkout | Approve & Pay |

**The primary CTA is always, everywhere, "Start Your Story".** Repetition is the point. A
visitor should encounter that phrase enough times that it becomes the obvious next action.

---

## 3. Brand line

```
SYS
Style Your Stories
```

Supporting one-liner, used in the footer and meta description:

> Custom-designed, made-to-measure fashion, created around your story, your body and your
> occasion.

Positioning sentence, used at the top of the app and in decks:

> Don't shop for your clothes. Create them.

The monochrome brand statement, used in the footer and wherever the design philosophy
is stated (a shorter alternate: *"You bring the story. We create what you wear."*):

> Your story is the colour. We create the form.

Attribution line, under the hero:

> Designed by [Designer Name] · Made in India · Custom Worldwide

## 5. Start Your Story — the flow

```
intro      Tell us your story.
           Six short questions. Two minutes. Then we'll put you in front of the designer.

01  What are we creating today?
      → occasion chips + "Something else"

02  Tell us a little more.
      placeholder: "My sister is getting married and I want something elegant but not
      too traditional."
      helper: Say it however you'd say it out loud. There's no right way.

03  Show us what inspires you.
      helper: A Pinterest image, an Instagram screenshot, an outfit you already own,
      a fabric, a celebrity look, or a rough sketch. Up to six.
      skip: I don't have anything to show yet

04  What's your style?
      Minimal · Elegant · Traditional · Contemporary · Dramatic · Romantic · Experimental
      helper: Choose as many as feel right.

05  What's your budget?
      ₹5,000–10,000 · ₹10,000–20,000 · ₹20,000–50,000 · ₹50,000+
      helper: This tells us which fabrics and how much handwork we can design toward.
      It is not a commitment.

06  When do you need it?
      Within a month · 1–3 months · 3+ months · I'm not sure yet
      helper: Custom garments generally need six to eight weeks. Tell us if yours is
      sooner and we'll say honestly whether we can.

→   Meet your designer.
      Your story is ready. Choose how you'd like to talk it through.
      [ Book a video consultation ]  [ Book a studio visit ]  [ Continue on WhatsApp ]
```

**Confirmation:**

```
Your story has begun.
[Designer Name] will read it herself and come back to you within one working day.
```

## 6. How It Works

```
eyebrow    THE PROCESS

headline   Your story → your garment.

standfirst Eight steps from the first message to the finished garment. Nothing
           happens that you haven't seen and approved.

01 — Tell us your story     Your occasion, your idea, your references.
02 — Consultation           A conversation, by video or in the studio.
03 — Design proposal        A sketch, a silhouette, a direction — and a price.
04 — Fabric & details       Fabric, colour, embroidery, finishings, chosen together.
05 — Measurements           Taken by the designer, kept for every future garment.
06 — First fitting          The garment on your body, adjusted where it should be.
07 — Final creation         Finishing, handwork, pressing, the last details.
08 — Delivered              Yours.

note       Timeline: six to eight weeks for most commissions. Couture takes longer,
           and we'll say so before you commit.
```
