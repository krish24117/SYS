# SYS — Strategy

**SYS — Style Your Stories**

> Don't shop for your clothes. Create them.

---

## 1. The positioning

SYS is a **premium, designer-led, custom fashion house**. It is not a boutique, not a
marketplace, and explicitly not a tailoring service.

The single sentence the whole business runs on:

> Your occasion. Your idea. Your designer. Your garment.

Everything — the website, the app, the pricing, the photography — must reinforce that a
customer is not selecting from inventory. They are commissioning a garment.

### What we sell

| We are selling | We are not selling |
| --- | --- |
| A process and a relationship | A rack of products |
| A designer's judgement | Anonymous production |
| A garment that could only belong to one person | A size chart |
| A memory turned into something wearable | A transaction |

### The name is the product

"Style Your Stories" is not decoration. It is the operating model:

- Enquiries are **Stories**, not leads.
- Orders are **Stories**, not order numbers.
- The portfolio is **Stories We've Styled**, not Products.
- The primary CTA is **Start Your Story**, never *Shop Now*, *Buy Now*, *Book Tailor*
  or *Get Stitching*.

That vocabulary is the difference between a fashion brand and a tailoring service. It is
enforced as a rule, not a preference — see `docs/03-copy-deck.md`.

## 2. Strategic sequencing

The fastest route to real customers and real revenue is **not** an e-commerce store. It is
a lead-generation and consultation engine.

### Phase 1 — Lead engine (now)

The website's job is to take a stranger from "this is beautiful" to "I have booked a
consultation" in one sitting. Revenue comes from consultations converting into commissions,
handled largely over WhatsApp and in the studio.

**Success metric:** qualified consultations booked per week, and their conversion to a
paid design fee.

### Phase 2 — Studio app

Once customers are actually paying, the operational load — measurements, design approvals,
fittings, production status, payments — becomes the bottleneck. The app removes it, for
both the customer and the designer.

**Success metric:** designer hours spent on admin per commission, trending down; repeat
order rate, trending up.

### Phase 3 — Design intelligence

AI-assisted visualization, fabric recognition, style profiles, virtual try-on. This layer
is only valuable once there is a body of real commissions to learn from — and it must never
replace the designer. It makes her more productive.

**Success metric:** design directions accepted without a re-brief.

The ordering matters. Building Phase 3 first produces a technology demo with no customers.
Building Phase 1 first produces customers who then justify the rest.

## 3. The moat

Three things compound, and none of them can be copied by a competitor with a better
photographer.

### 3.1 The designer is the brand

SYS must never feel like an anonymous fashion marketplace. The designer is the actual
creative asset, and she must be visible: her portrait, her philosophy, her sketches, her
fabric selection, her process. A named human being making judgement calls is the reason a
customer trusts a garment they cannot see yet.

### 3.2 The customer profile

After one completed garment, SYS holds something no competitor has: measurements, fit
preferences, colours they love, colours they avoid, silhouettes that worked, and the
history of what has already been made for them.

The second commission should be dramatically easier than the first. That is the retention
engine — SYS becomes *their designer*, not a vendor they re-evaluate each time.

### 3.3 Bring Your Fabric

The strongest differentiator available in the Indian market. A customer says:

> "I have my mother's saree."
> "I bought this fabric and I don't know what to do with it."

And SYS answers: *Let's create something from it.*

- Mother's saree → daughter's dress
- Old silk → contemporary gown
- Wedding saree → evening jacket
- Unused fabric → complete outfit

This is not a service line. It is the clearest possible proof of the brand promise: SYS
turns memories into fashion. It also generates the best stories the brand will ever tell.

## 4. Business model

Revenue is deliberately layered so that the business earns before production starts.

| # | Stream | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Design fee** | ₹500–₹5,000 by complexity. Paid up front. Qualifies the customer and pays for the designer's time even if the commission stops there. |
| 2 | **Stitching** | Margin on production. |
| 3 | **Fabric & materials** | Markup where SYS sources. |
| 4 | **Premium customization** | Embroidery, handwork, alterations. Highest-margin line. |
| 5 | **Repeat customers** | The compounding one. Profile + measurements + style history make every subsequent commission cheaper to serve. |

Payment structure: **50% advance on design approval, 50% before delivery.**

The design fee is the most important structural decision. It converts a free consultation
funnel into a paid one, filters unserious enquiries, and establishes from the first
interaction that design is the value — not stitching.

---

## 5. Pricing is public

Pricing must not be hidden. A new premium brand hiding its prices reads as either
expensive-and-embarrassed or cheap-and-pretending.

Published entry points (see `docs/03-copy-deck.md` for exact copy):

- Custom Design — from ₹X
- Custom Stitching — from ₹X
- Occasion Wear — from ₹X
- Couture — from ₹X

The purpose is **filtering**. Someone expecting a ₹2,000 tailoring service should
self-select out before they enter a premium custom-design process. Every such enquiry that
reaches the designer costs her time she cannot bill.

---

## 6. WhatsApp is the channel

In this market, and at this stage, WhatsApp is where the relationship actually happens.
Do not fight it.

- Never force a 15-field form. Every important page carries **Talk to SYS** and
  **Start on WhatsApp**.
- The guided flow's job is to arrive in WhatsApp with the story already written — occasion,
  references, style, budget, timeline — so the designer's first message is informed.
- Later, the app does not replace WhatsApp. It integrates: *"Your SYS design is ready for
  review"* → tap → app.

Reducing friction to first contact matters more than data capture completeness.

---

## 7. Visual identity — what we refuse

The visual language is the fastest signal that SYS is not a typical Indian boutique.

**Avoid:** excessive gold · maroon everywhere · heavy borders · wedding clichés · crowded
product grids · too many fonts · excessive jewellery imagery · generic "luxury fashion"
signalling.

**Instead:** editorial, minimal, warm, human, designer-led.

> Think: contemporary fashion magazine + private atelier.

Full tokens, type scale and rules in `docs/04-design-system.md`.

## 8. The signature visual language

The most compelling asset SYS can produce is not a product photograph. It is the
transformation, shown in four frames:

```
IDEA            DESIGN          CREATION              WEAR
inspiration  →  hand sketch  →  pattern / stitching →  the customer
photo,          by the          embroidery,           wearing the
fabric,         designer        construction          finished garment
sketch
```

Every completed commission should be captured this way. It sells the process, proves the
craft, and is impossible for a reseller to fake.

---

## 9. What success looks like at each stage

| Stage | The business is | The website is | The proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | A designer taking commissions | A lead engine | Consultations booked, design fees paid |
| Phase 2 | A studio with a system | A client portal | Admin hours down, repeat rate up |
| Phase 3 | A digital fashion platform | A design studio | Directions accepted first time |

The strategic bet: be a premium designer-led custom fashion business **first**. That gives
the fastest route to actual customers and revenue, while leaving every option open to
become a much larger digital fashion platform later.
