The process

Your story → your garment.

Eight steps from the first message to the finished garment. Nothing happens that you haven't seen and approved.

01 Tell us your story Your occasion, your idea, your references. Two minutes online, or a message on WhatsApp.
02 Consultation A conversation, by video or in the studio. We talk through what you want and what's realistic.
03 Design proposal A sketch, a silhouette, a direction — and a price, itemised, before anything is cut.
04 Fabric & details Fabric, colour, embroidery and finishings, chosen together. You approve each one.
05 Measurements Taken by the designer, and kept. Your next garment never asks for them again.
06 First fitting The garment on your body, adjusted where it should be rather than where a chart says.
07 Final creation Finishing, handwork, pressing, the last details.
08 Delivered Yours.

Timeline: six to eight weeks for most commissions. Couture takes longer, and we'll say so before you commit.

What it costs

Questions we're asked

Before you begin.

— Do I need to know what I want? No. “I don't know yet” is a perfectly good starting point — that's what the designer is for.
— Can I use my own fabric? Yes, and it's one of the things we do best. Bring Your Fabric.
— What if I'm not in Hyderabad? Consultations happen by video, measurements can be guided remotely, and we ship worldwide.
— How many fittings? Usually one for a simple garment, two or three for couture. We'd rather fit again than deliver something that's nearly right.
— What if I don't like the design? You approve the direction before any fabric is cut. Requesting changes at that stage is part of the process, not a complaint.
