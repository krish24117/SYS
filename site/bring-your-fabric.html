A transformation

Thirty years in a cupboard. Six weeks on a table.

01What she had

Her mother's wedding saree. Too precious to cut, too unworn to keep folded.

02The sketch

An evening jacket and a draped skirt, drawn around the border she loved.

03The making

Cut so the original zari survives at the hem, relined, nothing wasted.

04Worn again

Worn to her own daughter's engagement.

How it works

Send a photograph. That's the whole first step.

01 Photograph it In daylight, flat, with something for scale. A phone photo is fine.
02 We tell you honestly What the fabric can become, what it can't, and how much of it we'd need.
03 We design around it The silhouette follows the fabric, not the other way around.
04 Nothing is wasted Offcuts come back to you, or become a lining, a border, a second piece.
