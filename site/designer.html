Design philosophy

Three things she will not compromise on.

01

The person before the garment

The first conversation is never about fabric. It's about where you're going, who you'll be with, and how you want to feel by the end of the evening.

02

Proportion over decoration

Handwork is added because the silhouette asks for it, never to justify a price. A garment that needs embellishment to work isn't finished.

03

Wearable, or it failed

If you can't sit, eat, dance and be photographed in it, it isn't a success regardless of how it looks on a hanger.
